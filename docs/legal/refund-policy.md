# Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds may be issued for transactions processed through Arrabon (the "Service"). This Policy is incorporated into our Terms of Service.

---

## Overview

Arrabon is a blockchain-based escrow platform. When a Buyer funds a Consultation Link, USDC is transferred into a smart contract and held in escrow until one of the following outcomes occurs: the funds are released to the Seller, returned to the Buyer, or placed under review.

Because escrow transactions are processed on the Base blockchain, **all confirmed on-chain outcomes are final and irreversible.** Once funds have been released to the Seller, no refund is possible.

---

## When a Refund May Be Available

Refunds are not automatic. A refund requires an administrative resolution and is only possible while funds remain in escrow (before any release has occurred).

**1. No-show or non-delivery**

If a Seller does not appear or fails to deliver the consultation, a Buyer may open a dispute. If the dispute is resolved in the Buyer's favor following administrative review, funds will be returned to the Buyer's wallet.

**2. Disputed consultation quality**

If a Seller marks a consultation as completed but the Buyer disagrees, the Buyer has 48 hours to open a dispute. An administrator will review available evidence from both parties and determine whether funds should be returned to the Buyer or released to the Seller.

Refunds in quality disputes are not guaranteed. Each case is reviewed on its own merits.

---

## When a Refund Is Not Available

**Funds already released to Seller**

Once a release has occurred — whether through Buyer confirmation, automatic release after the 48-hour review window, or administrative resolution in the Seller's favor — the transaction is final. No refund can be issued after this point.

**Buyer chose not to dispute in time**

The dispute window is 48 hours from the moment a Seller marks a consultation as completed. If a Buyer does not open a dispute within this window, funds are automatically released to the Seller. This outcome cannot be reversed.

**Platform fees**

Platform fees applied within the escrow flow are non-refundable, regardless of the dispute outcome.

---

## Compliance-Related Holds and Restrictions

We conduct compliance and sanctions screening on wallet addresses involved in transactions. In certain circumstances, a transaction may be placed under review or restricted as a result of this screening.

If a transaction is placed under a compliance hold, funds cannot be released or refunded until the hold is resolved. In cases involving sanctions under applicable law — including designations by the U.S. Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) — we may be legally prohibited from returning funds without government authorization. We cannot provide a timeline for resolution in such cases.

If your transaction has been placed on hold, contact us at the address below for further information. We may not be able to disclose the specific basis for a compliance decision.

---

## How to Request a Refund

Refunds can only be requested through the dispute process available within the Service on the deal page. We do not accept refund requests submitted by email or other channels outside the platform.

To open a dispute:

1. Navigate to your deal page.
2. Use the dispute option available within the permitted time window.
3. Submit your dispute message and any supporting information.

An administrator will review your dispute and communicate a decision through the platform.
