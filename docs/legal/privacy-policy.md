# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how Arrabon, operated by its founders ("Arrabon," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares data in connection with our web application and all related products and services (the "Services"). Your use of the Services is subject to this Policy as well as our Terms of Service.

## High Level Summary

- Arrabon is a web application operated by its founders that enables single-use consultation payments using USDC on the Base blockchain.
- We collect wallet addresses, transaction data, and consultation details necessary to operate the Service. We also collect server-side technical data, including IP addresses, as part of routine logging.
- We conduct compliance and sanctions screening using third-party providers. Wallet addresses may be processed by those providers as part of this screening.
- We use strictly necessary session cookies for authentication. These cannot be disabled without affecting Service functionality.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, or similar identifying information unless you contact us directly.
- Blockchain transaction data is public and permanent. We cannot delete or modify on-chain records.
- Any material changes to this Policy will be reflected in an updated version posted to the Services.

## Data We Collect

**Wallet and Blockchain Information.** When you connect a wallet to the Services, we collect your publicly-available blockchain address to operate the Service and to screen your wallet for compliance and risk-control purposes. We may also collect blockchain transaction hashes and on-chain activity associated with your use of the Services, and smart contract interaction data relevant to consultation funding, release, dispute, refund, and related lifecycle actions. Note that blockchain addresses are publicly-available data not created or assigned by us, and by themselves are not personally identifying.

**Authentication and Session Information.** We collect Sign-In With Ethereum (SIWE) authentication data, nonce issuance and verification records, session identifiers, expiration data, and related security metadata necessary to maintain and protect authenticated sessions.

**Consultation and Transaction Information.** We collect consultation link details such as title, description, scheduled time, expiry, timezone, duration, and price; buyer and seller transaction relationships; dispute status, release and refund state, and related lifecycle records; and meeting URL data, which is stored in encrypted form and revealed only to authorized users.

**Compliance and Risk Information.** We collect wallet screening results, denylist and restricted-wallet records, and audit trail records related to sanctions, compliance, risk review, blocked flows, and administrative decisions, including provider responses or summaries relevant to compliance screening.

**Communications and User-Submitted Content.** We collect dispute messages submitted through the Service, evidence links or references you provide in dispute workflows, and any communications you send us directly through support or other channels.

**Technical and Usage Information.** We collect IP addresses, device and browser information, referring pages, operating system details, and request metadata as part of routine server-side logging necessary to operate, secure, and debug the Service. We may also collect information about how users interact with pages and product flows to the extent generated by the Services.

We use the data we collect in accordance with your instructions, including any applicable terms in our Terms of Service, and as required by law. We may also use data for the following purposes:

**Providing the Services.** We use data to provide, maintain, and operate the Services, including creating and managing consultation links, authenticating users, revealing protected meeting information to authorized users, and processing the full transaction lifecycle.

**Compliance and Risk Controls.** We use wallet addresses and transaction data to conduct sanctions screening, denylist checks, fraud prevention, and related compliance measures before or after funding or payout actions.

**Safety and Security.** We may use data to protect against, investigate, and stop fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity, to address security risks, enforce our agreements, and protect our users and the Services.

**Legal Compliance.** We may use data as needed or requested by regulators, government entities, and law enforcement to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

**Service Improvement.** We may use aggregated or anonymized data to understand how users use the Services and where we can improve the user experience.

## Compliance Screening

We may process wallet addresses and related transaction information for compliance and risk-control purposes, including sanctions screening, denylist checks, risk review, blocked transaction handling, and related audit logging. Compliance screening may be performed before or after funding and may result in refusal, delay, restriction, review, or blocking of certain actions. Screening may involve third-party compliance service providers that process wallet address data on our behalf under appropriate data processing arrangements.
